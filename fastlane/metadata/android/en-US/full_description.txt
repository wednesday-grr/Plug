<p>Plug is a fork of Gramophone by FoedusProgramme, enhanced with DJ Automix for local music playback.<br><br>- Beatmatching and beat-phase alignment for transitions<br>- Offline BPM and musical-key DSP analysis with a local cache<br>- Harmonic smart queue sorting<br>- Configurable Smooth Crossfade, Vinyl Brake, Club Bass-Swap, Filter Sweep, Echo Out, and Smart Auto transitions<br>- Offline playback for music stored on your device<br>- Material 3 design, playlists, synced lyrics, ReplayGain 2.0, and support for system or third-party equalizers<br><br>Automix analysis is an estimate, not a guaranteed beat grid. Embedded tags take precedence over cached analysis. Tracks without a stable modification date or a supported system decoder may be skipped.<br><br>Plug is open source and distributed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version. Plug retains the original Gramophone copyright notices and credits its authors and contributors. <a href="https://github.com/FoedusProgramme/Gramophone">FoedusProgramme/Gramophone upstream project</a><br><br>Plug is an offline player. It plays music files stored on your device and does not provide music downloads or streaming.</p>
