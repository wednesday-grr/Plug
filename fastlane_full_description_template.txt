<p>
Plug is an offline Android music player forked from Gramophone and enhanced with a real-time DJ Automix engine. It plays music stored on your device, including .mp3, .flac, .ogg, .opus, .m4a, .mka and .wav files.<br>
<br>
- Dual-deck DJ Automix with tempo matching and beat-phase alignment<br>
- Transition styles: Smooth Crossfade, Club Bass-Swap, Filter Sweep, BPM-Synced Echo Out, Vinyl Brake, Vocal-Swap and Smart Auto<br>
- Offline BPM, beat-grid and musical-key analysis, cached locally<br>
- Harmonic queue sorting using tempo and Camelot key compatibility<br>
- Light, stable and minimalistic<br>
- Up-to-date Material 3 design and theme<br>
- Dynamic player UI with Monet colors<br>
- Uses MediaStore to quickly access on-device music<br>
- Search and browse your favourite music<br>
- List and grid views<br>
- Browse songs by folder and filesystem<br>
- Natural sorting and other sorting options<br>
- Playlist support<br>
- Synced lyrics (LRC, TTML, SRT)<br>
- Supports word/syllable karaoke lyrics synchronization<br>
- Full support for ReplayGain 2.0<br>
- Support for system and third-party equalizer apps<br>
<br>

Note: Plug supports the "enhanced album covers" feature. This feature needs the "Photos and videos" permission. It is entirely optional and disabled by default. Plug does not upload data; the permission is only used to display album covers.<br>
<br>
Note: Not all audio file formats work on all devices. For more information, refer to <a href="https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/supported-formats#progressive-container">Media3 supported formats</a> and <a href="https://developer.android.com/media/platform/supported-formats#audio-formats">Android supported audio formats</a>.<br>
<br>
Note: Plug is completely offline and does not support downloading or streaming music. It plays music files on your device and cannot bypass copy protection.<br>
